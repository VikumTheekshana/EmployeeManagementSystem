# Sri Lankan Statutory Payroll & Tax Guidelines

## 1. Salary Disbursement Schedule
Salaries are calculated for each calendar month and credited directly to verified commercial bank accounts on or before the twenty-fifth (25th) of the active month. In the event the 25th falls on a weekend, statutory Poya holiday, or bank holiday, salaries are credited on the immediate preceding working day.

## 2. Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)
- Governed by the Employees' Provident Fund Act No. 15 of 1958 of Sri Lanka.
- Employee Mandatory Deduction: 8% of total qualifying earnings (Basic Salary + Fixed Allowances).
- Employer Mandatory Contribution: 12% of total qualifying earnings.

## 3. Employees' Trust Fund (ETF)
- Governed by the Employees' Trust Fund Act No. 46 of 1980 of Sri Lanka.
- Employer Mandatory Contribution: 3% of total qualifying earnings.
- This is entirely paid by the company and is never deducted from the employee's gross earnings.

## 4. Advance Personal Income Tax (APIT)
- Collected under Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) progressive tax guidelines issued by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Sri Lanka.
- Up to LKR 100,000 monthly earnings: 0% Tax (Tax-free exemption threshold).
- Incremental monthly brackets of LKR 41,667 are taxed progressively at 6%, 12%, 18%, 24%, 30%, and 36% for earnings exceeding LKR 308,333.
- Annual Withholding Tax / APIT certificates (T-10 certificates) are issued automatically by the HR portal by March 31st each year.

## 5. Payslips
Digital payslips signed cryptographically are generated upon payroll approval and accessible securely in the Employee Self-Service portal.
