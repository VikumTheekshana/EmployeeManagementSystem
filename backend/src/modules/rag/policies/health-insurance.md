# Corporate Health & Medical Insurance Policy

## 1. Eligibility & Coverage Overview
All permanent employees and their legal dependents (spouse and up to two children under age 21) are enrolled in the Corporate Health Insurance scheme from their first day of permanent employment.

## 2. Outpatient (OPD) Benefits
- Annual Limit: Up to LKR 75,000 per policy year per employee.
- Covered Expenses: General Physician consultations, specialist consultations, diagnostic lab tests, prescription drugs from licensed pharmacies, dental pain management, and optical check-ups.
- Optical Specs Allowance: Up to LKR 15,000 per year within the total OPD limit.

## 3. Hospitalization (Inpatient) Benefits
- Annual Limit: Up to LKR 750,000 per family per policy year.
- Room Category: Up to LKR 18,000 per day in private accredited hospitals in Sri Lanka (e.g. Asiri, Lanka Hospitals, Nawaloka, Kings Hospital).
- Pre- and Post-Hospitalization Expenses: Covered up to 30 days prior and 30 days post-discharge.

## 4. Critical Illness Cover
- Lump sum benefit of LKR 1,000,000 upon verified diagnosis of listed critical conditions including cardiac surgery, cancer, organ transplant, and stroke.

## 5. Claims Submission Procedure
- Cashless Hospitalization: Available via presenting the Corporate Digital Medical Card at registered partner hospital insurance counters.
- Reimbursement Claims: All bills, pharmacy receipts, and doctor referral forms must be uploaded through the HRMS Document Vault within fourteen (14) calendar days of treatment.
