# Corporate Leave & Absence Policy

## 1. Statutory Compliance
This policy operates in full compliance with the Shop and Office Employees Act No. 19 of 1954 and subsequent amendments in Sri Lanka.

## 2. Annual Leave
- Every permanent employee is entitled to fourteen (14) days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- In the first year of employment, annual leave is prorated based on the quarter of joining.
- A maximum of seven (7) days of unutilized annual leave may be carried forward to the following calendar year. Any excess will lapse automatically on December 31st.

## 3. Casual Leave
- Employees are granted seven (7) days of paid casual leave per calendar year.
- Casual leave is intended for unanticipated personal emergencies or urgent private affairs.
- Casual leave cannot be taken for more than two (2) consecutive days without prior approval from the reporting manager.
- Casual leave cannot be accumulated or carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.

## 4. Medical / Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to fourteen (14) days of paid medical leave per calendar year.
- If an employee is absent on medical grounds for more than two (2) consecutive days, a valid Medical Certificate (MC) issued by a registered medical practitioner (SLMC registered) must be submitted upon resuming duties.

## 5. Maternity Leave
- Female employees are entitled to eighty-four (84) working days of paid maternity leave for each confinement.
- Maternity leave applies regardless of the number of surviving children, adhering to enhanced national welfare standards.

## 6. No-Pay Leave
- Leave taken without available balance is classified as No-Pay Leave.
- For each day of No-Pay Leave, basic salary deduction is calculated as: `(Basic Salary / 30) * NoPayDays`.
