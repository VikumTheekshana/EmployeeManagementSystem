# Employee Separation, Notice Periods & Statutory Gratuity

## 1. Notice Periods
- Employees on Probation: One (1) month written notice or one month's gross salary in lieu of notice.
- Confirmed Permanent Employees: Two (2) months written notice or payment of equivalent salary in lieu of notice.
- Notice periods may be waived or shortened only with mutual written consent from the Department Head and Head of HR.

## 2. Company Asset Recovery Gate
Prior to the final working day, all corporate assets must be surrendered to the IT Department:
- Laptops, monitors, power adapters, and peripherals.
- Corporate access keycards, tokens, and encrypted storage media.
- Company corporate SIM cards and smart devices.
Upon physical inspection, the IT Department will sign off on the IT Clearance Gate in the HRMS.

## 3. Sri Lankan Payment of Gratuity Act
- Under the Payment of Gratuity Act No. 12 of 1983 of Sri Lanka, any employee who has completed not less than five (5) continuous years of service is legally entitled to terminal gratuity upon resignation, retirement, or termination.
- Gratuity Rate: Half (1/2) a month's basic salary for every completed year of service, calculated based on the last drawn basic salary.
- Formula: `Gratuity Payable = (Last Basic Salary / 2) * CompletedYearsOfService`.
- Gratuity payments must be released within thirty (30) days from the last date of employment.

## 4. Final Settlement & Service Letter
Upon successful clearance across IT, Finance, and HR gates:
- Final dues, encashment of unutilized annual leave (if applicable), and gratuity are transferred via direct bank deposit.
- An official Certificate of Service & Relieving Letter is released immediately.
